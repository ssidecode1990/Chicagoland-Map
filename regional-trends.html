All regional figures are population-weighted aggregates (not simple averages of individual towns), computed the same way as the table above. Charts on this page exclude 2020 as a checkpoint year — the Census Bureau changed how it codes race/ethnicity write-in responses starting with the 2020 Decennial Census, producing a documented shift independent of real demographic change, and that discontinuity would otherwise show up as a spurious swing in every chart below.
